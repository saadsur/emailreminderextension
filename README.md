Email Reminder Checklist lets you save important emails as to-do items with one click — directly from Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, ProtonMail, Fastmail, Zoho Mail, or any corporate webmail.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open any email in your webmail client.
2. Click the 'Save Reminder' button that appears in the bottom-right corner of the page.
3. The email subject and date are saved as a reminder.
4. Open the extension popup (toolbar icon) to see, check off, or delete reminders.
5. Every new tab shows your morning dashboard with a live clock and your full checklist.

FEATURES
- Works on Gmail, Outlook (live, office, office365, cloud), Yahoo Mail, ProtonMail, Fastmail, Zoho Mail, and more
- Smart tagging: automatically labels emails as Urgent, Follow-up, Later, or General
- Action item detection: scans email body for action phrases
- Progress bar showing how many tasks you have handled
- Morning notification at 8:00 AM if you have pending tasks
- All data stored locally on your device — nothing is sent to any server
- Works with ad blockers enabled

PRIVACY
This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All reminders are stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts required.

PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED
- Storage: to save your reminders locally on your device
- Alarms: to trigger the 8 AM morning notification
- Notifications: to show the morning reminder alert
- Tabs: to open the full dashboard view when you click 'Full View'
- Scripting: to inject the Save Reminder button on webmail pages
